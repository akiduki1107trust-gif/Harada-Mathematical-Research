**Archived release:** [GitHub v0.1](https://github.com/akiduki1107trust-gif/Harada-Mathematical-Research/releases/tag/v0.1) | **Version DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23255029](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23255029)

**Author:** Shigehisa Harada (原田重壽) — Independent Researcher

**Research status:** Preliminary research program; no solution to the Riemann hypothesis, P vs NP, a complete theory of everything, or a new aperiodic monotile is claimed.

This repository hosts technical reports, definitions, candidate mathematical constructions, partial results, open proof obligations, and verification materials in five areas:

- **RH / HBP:** prime-valuation operators, localized Weil positivity, pole-neutral KKT/Schur certification.
- **HUM:** proposed spectral regularization and ten-to-four-dimensional mathematical physics.
- **NCHO:** noncommutative harmonic oscillator, spectral zeta functions, Laurent residues, and metaplectic methods.
- **PHR-LTC:** exploratory pair/repair/cut ideas in complexity and proof systems.
- **FS57:** an explicit 57-degree iterated function system, 8-adic symbolic dynamics, and a proposed tiling-rigidity bridge.

We distinguish **ESTABLISHED / STANDARD**, **INTERNAL PASS — AUDIT PENDING**, **CHECK**, **OPEN**, and **RETRACTED / NO-GO**. A result recorded in an internal handoff is not automatically a verified theorem. Published mathematical foundations are credited in the reports.
